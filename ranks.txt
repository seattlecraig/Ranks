=====================================
RANKS GUIDE  
=====================================
A player's guide to the Ranks progression system
By SupaFloof Games
=====================================
PAGE 1
=====================================
WHAT IS RANKS?

Ranks is a progression system that lets you advance through different ranks by spending in-game currency.

As you earn money on the server, you can purchase higher ranks that unlock new permissions and perks.
=====================================
PAGE 2
=====================================
GETTING STARTED

When you first join, you'll automatically be assigned to the starting rank.

From there, you can work your way up by earning money and purchasing rank upgrades.
=====================================
PAGE 3
=====================================
VIEWING RANKS

Command: /ranks

This shows all available ranks with:
- Your current rank (green arrow)
- Completed ranks (✓)
- Future ranks (✗)
- Cost of each rank
- Your balance
- How much more you need
=====================================
PAGE 4
=====================================
UPGRADE TO NEXT RANK

Command: /ranks next

This upgrades you to the next rank in progression.

The plugin will:
1. Check if you can afford it
2. Withdraw the money
3. Give you the new rank
4. Announce to the server
=====================================
PAGE 5
=====================================
SKIP MULTIPLE RANKS

Command: /ranks upgrade <rankname>

Example: /ranks upgrade hero

This lets you jump ahead by paying the combined cost of all ranks in between.
=====================================
PAGE 6
=====================================
RANK COSTS

Free Ranks (Cost: $0)
- Usually the starting rank

Purchasable Ranks
- Have a specific money cost
- Buy with in-game currency

Non-Purchasable Ranks
- Cannot be bought with money
- Usually donor ranks
=====================================
PAGE 7
=====================================
GETTING HELP

Command: /ranks help

Shows all available commands with descriptions.

If you need more help, ask a server administrator!
=====================================
PAGE 8
=====================================
UNDERSTANDING YOUR DISPLAY

When you use /ranks, you'll see:

✓ [Mortal] - $0
✓ [Adept] - $40,000 <----
✗ [Hero] - $200,000

✓ = Completed
✗ = Not yet achieved
<---- = Your current rank
=====================================
PAGE 9
=====================================
WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU RANK UP?

1. Money is deducted from your account

2. A broadcast announces your achievement

3. Your permissions update automatically

4. You gain access to new perks
=====================================
PAGE 10
=====================================
COMMON ERRORS

"You don't have enough money!"
- Earn more currency first

"This rank cannot be purchased with currency"
- This is a donor/premium rank

"You are already at the highest rank!"
- Congratulations!
=====================================
PAGE 11
=====================================
TIPS FOR SUCCESS

- Check /ranks regularly to track your progress

- Save up for multi-rank jumps if you want

- Plan which rank you want to reach

- Earn money through server activities
=====================================
PAGE 12
=====================================
WHEN TO USE EACH COMMAND

Use /ranks next when:
- Upgrading one at a time
- You're close to the next rank
- You want each celebration

Use /ranks upgrade when:
- You've saved up lots of money
- You want to skip ahead
- Reaching a goal rank quickly
=====================================
PAGE 13
=====================================
CHECKING AFFORDABILITY

The /ranks display shows if you can afford each rank:

"(Can afford)" in green
- You have enough money

"(Need $X more)" in red
- How much more you need

This helps you plan!
=====================================
PAGE 14
=====================================
RANK PROGRESSION

Ranks always go upward!

You cannot:
- Downgrade to lower ranks
- Buy the same rank twice
- Skip non-purchasable ranks

But you can:
- Jump multiple ranks at once
- View all ranks anytime
=====================================
PAGE 15
=====================================
ABOUT BROADCASTS

When you rank up, all online players see a message!

This celebrates your achievement and lets everyone know about your progress.

Broadcasts are automatic and configured by the server.
=====================================
PAGE 16
=====================================
MULTI-RANK UPGRADES

When you jump multiple ranks:

- You pay the TOTAL cost
- All ranks in between process
- You get all permissions
- Each rank announces

Example: Mortal to Hero pays for both Adept and Hero!
=====================================
PAGE 17
=====================================
YOUR BALANCE MATTERS

Always check your balance before upgrading!

The plugin tells you:
- Current balance
- Rank costs
- How much more needed

No money is taken unless the upgrade succeeds.
=====================================
PAGE 18
=====================================
RANK NAMES

Rank names are not case-sensitive.

These all work:
/ranks upgrade hero
/ranks upgrade Hero
/ranks upgrade HERO

Just type it correctly!
=====================================
PAGE 19
=====================================
AUTOMATIC PERMISSIONS

When you rank up, the server automatically:

- Adds your new rank permissions
- Updates your group
- Removes old rank group

You don't need to do anything - it's automatic!
=====================================
PAGE 20
=====================================
ENJOYING YOUR JOURNEY

Ranking up is part of your server experience!

Take your time, enjoy the progression, and celebrate each achievement.

Good luck on your journey to the top!
